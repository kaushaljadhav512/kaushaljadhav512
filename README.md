#### Featured projects

- **[diffusion-planner-scorer](https://github.com/kaushaljadhav512/diffusion-planner-scorer)**: adds best-of-K sampling and learned trajectory selection to Diffusion Planner. It improves the nuPlan Test14-hard closed-loop score by 2.3 points, and its transformer verifier scores candidates in about 1 ms, versus 36 ms for PDM.
- **[forge_blocks](https://github.com/kaushaljadhav512/forge_blocks)**: sim-to-real, force-guided block insertion on a Franka arm. Reproduces FORGE in Isaac Lab as a baseline for adding moment feedback.
- **[moveit_cartesian](https://github.com/kaushaljadhav512/moveit_cartesian)**: constant-velocity Cartesian motion planning for a UR10e on a vertical lift, with ROS 2 and MoveIt 2.
- **[Algorithmic-Trading](https://github.com/kaushaljadhav512/Algorithmic-Trading)**: 🥈 first runner-up in WnCC Codewars at IIT Bombay, with a mean-reversion trading bot for a simulated market.
